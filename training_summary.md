## Honest limitations

- The target is right-censored at 100 points (26.8 % of rows), and the only strong predictor is weekly study hours (r = 0.81). Attendance and participation carry almost no signal in this synthetic table, so R² is bounded near 0.66.
- Grade is a near-deterministic binning of the score (99.79 % agreement at the documented cut-offs); the grade model therefore learns the noisy boundary behaviour from behaviour features and is useful only as a coarse indicator.
- These are tabular tree/MLP models, not language models. The natural-language demo is a template/retrieval layer over them, and it labels observations vs predictions explicitly.
